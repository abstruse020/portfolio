Designed a scalable heterogeneous GNN explainability algorithm for real-world fraud detection on graphs exceeding 150 million nodes. Extended the algorithm to handle million-scale graphs and graph heterogeneity while identifying and fixing key failure modes.

## Impact

* Improved explainability fidelity with respect to the underlying GNN from **52% to 82%**.
* Developed the approach to operate on large-scale heterogeneous graphs.
