Proposed a Global Explainer that generates natural-language rules for Graph Neural Networks using Large Language Models, enabling interpretable explanations of GNN behavior.

## Research

* Led core model design and experimental evaluation from the Fujitsu Research side.
* Developed in collaboration with IIT Delhi.
* Accepted as an **Oral Paper at NeurIPS 2025**.
